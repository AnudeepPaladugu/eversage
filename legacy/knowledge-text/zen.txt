Soto Zen
Shobogenzo — Genjokoan
Original reflections, not quotations.
Source context: Dogen connects studying the way with studying the self and letting go of a narrow focus on the self.
Source link: https://www.sotozen.com/eng/practice/sutra/index.html

CAREER
You can bring full care to your work without making every task a test of your worth.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan
Choose one useful task and give it ten minutes of calm effort.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan

RELATIONSHIPS
Let listening take the place of preparing your next defence.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan
Write one kind sentence that also makes your needs clear.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan

FAMILY
An ordinary shared task can become a quiet act of care.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan
Choose a quiet time to share one need and listen to one reply.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan

STRESS
This small moment is enough space in which to begin again.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan
Pause for three slow breaths, then choose one small next step.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan

LOSS
You can be present with grief without needing to turn it into a lesson today.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan
Give yourself time today to remember, rest, or speak with someone you trust.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan

PURPOSE
Meaning can be practised in the way you meet the life in front of you.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan
Write down one value you want your next action to show.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan

CONFIDENCE
You do not need to perform a perfect self before you can take part in life.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan
Name one small thing you have learned and one thing you can practise.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan

FRIENDSHIP
A simple, attentive meeting can be a meaningful gift to a friend.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan
Send one honest and kind message to someone you would like to connect with.
Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan

DECISIONS

Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan

HABITS

Inspired by Shobogenzo · Genjokoan
