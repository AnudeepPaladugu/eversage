Bhagavad Gita — Chapter 2, verses 47–48
Original reflections, not quotations.
Source context: Krishna speaks to Arjuna about action, attachment to results, and steadiness in success or failure.
Source link: https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/2/verse/47

CAREER

Inspired by Bhagavad Gita · Chapter 2, verses 47–48
