Latter-day Saint Christianity
Book of Mormon — Mosiah 2:17
Original reflections, not quotations.
Source context: King Benjamin connects service to other people with service to God.
Source link: https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/2?lang=eng&id=p17

CAREER
You can find meaning in the part of your work that genuinely helps someone.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17
Choose one useful task and give it ten minutes of calm effort.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17

RELATIONSHIPS
A small, freely chosen act of help can express care more clearly than a promise.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17
Write one kind sentence that also makes your needs clear.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17

FAMILY
Look for one practical way to support a family member while respecting your own needs.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17
Choose a quiet time to share one need and listen to one reply.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17

STRESS
A small act of service can give your next step a clear purpose.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17
Pause for three slow breaths, then choose one small next step.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17

LOSS
You can accept help as well as offer it while moving through grief.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17
Give yourself time today to remember, rest, or speak with someone you trust.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17

PURPOSE
You can explore purpose through the people your actions help.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17
Write down one value you want your next action to show.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17

CONFIDENCE
You have something useful to offer, even when your confidence feels small.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17
Name one small thing you have learned and one thing you can practise.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17

FRIENDSHIP
A simple act of help can be a quiet way to show friendship.
Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17

Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17

DECISIONS

Inspired by Book of Mormon · Mosiah 2:17
