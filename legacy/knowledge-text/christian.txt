Christianity
Bible — Matthew 6:25–34
Original reflections, not quotations.
Source context: Jesus speaks about worry, daily needs, and seeking God’s Kingdom. The passage turns attention away from anxious concern about tomorrow.
Source link: https://ebible.org/web/MAT06.htm

CAREER
You can work with care today without asking yourself to carry every future result.
Inspired by Bible · Matthew 6:25–34
