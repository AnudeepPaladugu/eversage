Stoicism
Enchiridion — Section 1
Original reflections, not quotations.
Source context: Epictetus distinguishes what is within our control from things that are not fully ours to command.
Source link: https://classics.mit.edu/Epictetus/epicench.html

CAREER
Your preparation is yours to shape, even when the final decision belongs to someone else.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Choose one useful task and give it ten minutes of calm effort.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1

RELATIONSHIPS
You can choose an honest response without choosing another person’s feelings for them.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Write one kind sentence that also makes your needs clear.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1

FAMILY
You can care for your part in a family conversation without carrying everyone’s reactions.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Choose a quiet time to share one need and listen to one reply.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1

STRESS
Bring your effort back to one thing that is actually within your control.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Pause for three slow breaths, then choose one small next step.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1

LOSS
You can choose a gentle response to this day without controlling the fact of change.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Give yourself time today to remember, rest, or speak with someone you trust.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1

PURPOSE
Your own choices give you a place to begin living by your values.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Write down one value you want your next action to show.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1

CONFIDENCE
Let your standards for effort guide you more than an opinion you cannot command.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Name one small thing you have learned and one thing you can practise.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1

FRIENDSHIP
You can offer friendship honestly while leaving room for another person’s choice.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Send one honest and kind message to someone you would like to connect with.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1

DECISIONS
Separate what you can choose from what you can only take into account.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Write down your options and one value that can guide your choice.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1

HABITS
Give your attention to the next repetition, which is yours to attempt.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
Choose one small action you can repeat at a clear time tomorrow.
Inspired by Enchiridion · Section 1
