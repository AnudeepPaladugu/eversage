Theravada Buddhism
Dhammapada — Chapter 2, verses 21–23
Original reflections, not quotations.
Source context: These verses praise heedfulness: a careful, steady way of living and practising.
Source link: https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/dhp/dhp.02.than.html

CAREER
Steady attention to one task can help you move forward at your own pace.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23
Choose one useful task and give it ten minutes of calm effort.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23

RELATIONSHIPS
Listening carefully is a useful act of care in a close relationship.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23
Write one kind sentence that also makes your needs clear.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23

FAMILY
You can bring patience to the next small moment with your family.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23
Choose a quiet time to share one need and listen to one reply.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23

STRESS
Return gently to what is happening now, one moment at a time.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23
Pause for three slow breaths, then choose one small next step.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23

LOSS
Give this day your care without asking yourself to feel ready for every tomorrow.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23
Give yourself time today to remember, rest, or speak with someone you trust.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23

PURPOSE
You can begin living by a value through one attentive action today.
Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23

Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23

CONFIDENCE

Inspired by Dhammapada · Chapter 2, verses 21–23

FRIENDSHIP
