Zoroastrianism
Avesta — Yasna 30.2–3
Original reflections, not quotations.
Source context: The hymn calls listeners to hear, consider with clear thought, and choose between better and harmful ways of thinking, speaking, and acting.
Source link: https://www.avesta.org/yasna/index.html

CAREER
You can choose work that reflects care in both its purpose and its method.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3
Choose one useful task and give it ten minutes of calm effort.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3

RELATIONSHIPS
Give your words a moment of clear thought before you send them.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3
Write one kind sentence that also makes your needs clear.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3

FAMILY
You can bring thoughtful words and useful actions to a family concern.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3
Choose a quiet time to share one need and listen to one reply.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3

STRESS
A clear look at one choice can be more useful than wrestling with every fear.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3
Pause for three slow breaths, then choose one small next step.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3

LOSS
You can choose a caring next action while allowing your feelings to be real.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3
Give yourself time today to remember, rest, or speak with someone you trust.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3

PURPOSE
Your values can become visible through the thoughts, words, and actions you choose.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3
Write down one value you want your next action to show.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3

CONFIDENCE
A thoughtful choice is a real strength you can practise today.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3
Name one small thing you have learned and one thing you can practise.
Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3

FRIENDSHIP

Inspired by Avesta · Yasna 30.2–3
