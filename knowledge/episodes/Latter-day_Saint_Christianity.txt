Source-based retellings; original applications are not scripture quotations.

Nephi makes a new bow after the old one breaks
Source: Book of Mormon — 1 Nephi 16:18–32
Type: Scripture narrative
Situation: A tool or plan I depended on has failed.
What happened: Nephi’s bow breaks while the travelling family needs food. Amid the group’s distress, he makes a wooden bow and an arrow, then asks his father where he should go to obtain food.
Teaching: The episode combines making a practical replacement with seeking guidance.
Why it fits: This fits a failed method or resource, not a promise that the loss has no cost.
Application: When the old method fails, separate the need from the tool that used to meet it. A workable replacement may be enough for the next stage.
Try: Write the need the failed tool or plan served. List one available substitute and the guidance you need to use it.

Alma tells of guilt, remembrance and turning for help
Source: Book of Mormon — Alma 36:6–21
Type: Scripture narrative
Situation: I feel overwhelmed by regret about what I have done.
What happened: Alma recounts distress over his wrongdoing and his remembrance of teaching about Jesus Christ. He describes turning to Christ for mercy and a profound change in his experience.
Teaching: The account presents repentance within faith, not denial of the wrong that caused the remorse.
Why it fits: This fits remorse about your own conduct, not shame imposed by someone else.
Application: You can seek spiritual help while also considering what repair and changed conduct are possible. The passage is not a promise of instant relief for every reader.
Try: Speak honestly with a trusted spiritual adviser or support person about one part you can take responsibility for.
