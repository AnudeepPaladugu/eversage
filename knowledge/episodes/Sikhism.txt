A hymn opens a different way of seeing others
Source: Guru Granth Sahib — Ang 1299; Guru Arjan’s hymn beginning Na ko bairi nahi begana
Type: Scriptural hymn
Situation: I have begun to see everyone around me as an enemy.
What happened: In this hymn, Guru Arjan describes a changed relationship with others: no enemy and no stranger, within a vision shaped by the Divine. It is a spiritual perspective, not a report of a modern social dispute.
Teaching: The hymn loosens a fixed division between oneself and others.
Why it fits: This fits blanket suspicion or a hardened social division, not a demand to trust unsafe people.
Application: You can question whether every person has become an enemy in your mind while still recognising real danger and keeping necessary boundaries.
Try: Choose one safe interaction in which you can replace an assumption with a genuine question.

Sweetness and humility are presented as strengths
Source: Guru Granth Sahib — Ang 470; Asa Ki Var, salok on sweetness and humility
Type: Scriptural teaching
Situation: I want to speak clearly without making a family argument worse.
What happened: The salok praises sweetness and humility as qualities with real value. It appears within Asa Ki Var’s examination of conduct, not as advice to hide the truth.
Teaching: The teaching directs attention to the manner in which one speaks and lives.
Why it fits: This fits the way a difficult conversation is being handled.
Application: A respectful tone does not need to erase your request. You can name the issue plainly without humiliating the other person.
Try: Prepare one sentence that describes the issue and one that says what you need, without an insult.
