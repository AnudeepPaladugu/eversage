The narrator begins by asking what he wants to understand
Source: Corpus Hermeticum — Treatise I, Poimandres, opening dialogue
Type: Visionary teaching dialogue
Situation: I feel lost because I have too many unanswered questions.
What happened: The narrator describes being absorbed in thought when Poimandres appears and asks what he wishes to hear and learn. The dialogue begins with a stated wish to understand existence and the divine.
Teaching: The text frames a spiritual search through questions and instruction.
Why it fits: This fits an unfocused search for understanding, rather than a concrete emergency.
Application: You need not solve your whole life in one question. Give your search a clear starting point without treating this visionary dialogue as a modern psychological method.
Try: Choose the question that most affects your next decision and one trustworthy source or person through whom to examine it.
