Moses learns to hear the shepherd’s devotion
Source: Masnavi — Book II, story of Moses and the Shepherd
Type: Spiritual parable
Situation: I am afraid that my honest words are not good enough.
What happened: In Rumi’s parable, Moses rebukes a shepherd’s very human way of speaking to God. A divine rebuke then challenges Moses for separating a devoted person from God. Moses goes back to find the shepherd.
Teaching: The parable contrasts attention to outward wording with attention to sincere devotion.
Why it fits: This fits shame about imperfect expression, not permission to disregard another person’s beliefs.
Application: If fear of saying things perfectly is stopping an honest conversation or prayer, begin with sincere words. This is Rumi’s spiritual parable, not an account from the Quran.
Try: Write what you honestly mean before editing it to impress someone.
