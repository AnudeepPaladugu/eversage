Tamil ethical wisdom
Source-based retellings; original applications are not scripture quotations.

Tiruvalluvar places thought before commitment
Source: Tirukkural — Chapter 47, especially couplet 467
Type: Ethical teaching
Situation: I am about to commit because I feel rushed.
What happened: In the chapter on acting after deliberation, Tiruvalluvar places careful consideration before undertaking an action. Couplet 467 warns against treating reflection as something to begin only after committing.
Teaching: The teaching concerns the timing of thought in relation to action.
Why it fits: This fits a rushed commitment, rather than general low confidence.
Application: For an important choice, look at the conditions and consequences before giving your agreement. This is different from postponing forever after enough thought.
Try: List two likely consequences and one missing fact you should check before committing.

Kind speech is tested against needless harshness
Source: Tirukkural — Chapter 10, couplets 91–100
Type: Ethical teaching
Situation: I need to say something difficult without hurting someone needlessly.
What happened: This chapter examines sincere and pleasant speech. It values words shaped by care and contrasts them with harshness chosen when a kinder way is available.
Teaching: The teaching addresses how truth and care can shape speech together.
Why it fits: This fits the wording of a difficult conversation, not an obligation to please everyone.
Application: You can keep the necessary truth while removing a needless insult or humiliation. Kindness does not require hiding the issue.
Try: Write the sentence you need to say, then remove the part that attacks the person rather than names the problem.
